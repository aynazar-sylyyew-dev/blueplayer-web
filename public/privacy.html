Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Last updated: 8 November 2026 · Published by Seydi Charyyev · App developed by Aýnazar Sylyýew

Summary

We do not collect, receive or store your personal data on our servers. Blue Player is a local music player. It has no accounts, no registration, no advertising, no payments and no analytics. Your music library, playlists, favorites, bookmarks, play statistics and settings are stored only on your device and never leave it unless you turn Internet access on.

Internet access is off by default. While it is off, Blue Player sends no requests to any server and shows no images from the internet. If you turn it on in Settings → Advanced, the track title and artist name, or only the artist name for artist images, are sent to the lookup services listed below. Those services can see your IP address. We do not receive, store or sell this data.

Data collection, sharing, storage and deletion

This section maps directly to the Google Play Data Safety declarations for this app.

Category What we do
Collected by us None. We operate no servers that receive your data. There is no account, no upload, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising ID.
Shared with third parties Only when you turn Internet access on: the track title and artist name (for cover art) or the artist name only (for artist images) are sent to the lookup services listed below. These requests are made directly from your device to those services; we never see or store them.
Stored on device Your library index, playlists, favorites, bookmarks, play statistics, app and equalizer settings, and downloaded cover/artist images. See the next section.
Secured in transit All network requests use HTTPS.
Retention Device data exists only while the app is installed. We retain nothing on our side, because we receive nothing.
Deletion Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device immediately. Cover and image caches can be cleared in Settings → Advanced. Because we hold no server-side data, there is nothing for us to delete on request — but you can still write to us at the contact below and we will confirm in writing that we hold no data about you.

Data stored on your device

  • Library index: track titles, artists, albums, durations and the locations of your music files.
  • Playlists, favorites, bookmarks (saved playback positions) and the last playback queue.
  • Play statistics: how often and when each track was played. They power the Library categories "Recent", "Top" and "Forgotten".
  • App settings and equalizer settings.
  • Downloaded cover art and artist images, plus any custom album art you choose.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device. Cover and image caches can be cleared in Settings → Advanced.

Permissions and why we request them

We follow the Google Play minimum scope principle: every permission is requested only for a core feature, and we do not use broad storage access. In particular, Blue Player does not use MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (All Files Access) — it reads only audio through the scoped media APIs.

Permission Why it is used
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12L and older) Finds and plays the music stored on your device.
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 13 and newer) Finds and plays the music stored on your device.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 10 and older) Used only when you delete a track or save tag changes. You start both actions yourself.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows playback controls in the notification shade.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Runs playback as a foreground service, so music keeps playing when the screen is off or the app is in the background.
WAKE_LOCK Keeps the device awake while music is playing.
INTERNET Looks up online covers and artist images, only when Internet access is turned on.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks whether you are on Wi-Fi, for the Wi-Fi-only option.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores the home screen widget after the device restarts. It reads no personal data.

Internet access, online covers and artist images

Internet access is off by default (Settings → Advanced). While it is off, Blue Player sends no requests and does not show any image from the internet. You can also limit Internet access to Wi-Fi only; this option is off by default. The Wi-Fi-only setting applies to all the lookups and downloads described here. All lookups and downloads use HTTPS.

Images are shown only after Blue Player has downloaded them to your device. When Internet access is on, the app looks up:

  • Cover art for the track that is playing, and in the background for tracks in your queue, your favorites and your library. These lookups also require the Online covers switch, which is on by default. The data sent is the track title and artist name.
  • Artist images for the artist of the track that is playing, for each artist shown on the Artists screen, and on an artist page. The Online covers switch does not apply. The data sent is the artist name only.
  • Cover art on the track information screen for the track you are viewing. This screen follows Internet access and the Wi-Fi-only setting only; the Online covers switch does not apply. The app requests the image address of the track.

Downloading an image also contacts the image server of the same service. The images are saved on your device. Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address, and they handle requests under their own privacy policies.

The services that receive these requests are:

  • Apple iTunes Search API (Apple Inc.) — privacy policy
  • Deezer — privacy policy
  • MusicBrainz (MetaBrainz Foundation) — privacy policy
  • Cover Art Archive (a joint project of the Internet Archive and MusicBrainz) — privacy information
  • Last.fm — privacy policy
  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) — privacy policy

Editing tags, deleting and sharing files

Tag editing and file deletion work only on files you select, and only when you start the action. Changes are made directly to the file on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Sharing a track opens the standard Android share sheet, and you choose the receiving app.

Backups

Blue Player is excluded from Android's automatic backup, so your data is not copied to your Google account. Settings → Advanced lets you export and import app settings as a file you choose. The file stays on your device unless you share it yourself. The app never uploads it.

Third-party services

Blue Player does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The only services it contacts are the lookup services listed above, and only when Internet access is on.

The About and Libraries screens contain links to the project page on GitHub and to the websites of the libraries the app uses. These links open in your browser only when you tap them. Blue Player does not send requests to them.

Data security

There is no server to breach: we do not transmit or store your personal data. Device-side data is protected by your device's own encryption and lock screen. Every network request the app can make (cover and artist lookup, only when enabled) is sent over HTTPS.

Children's privacy

Blue Player is not directed at children. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases, so it is not subject to the Families policies' data-handling requirements beyond what is stated here.

Your choices and rights

  • Keep Internet access off, or allow it on Wi-Fi only (Settings → Advanced).
  • Turn off the Online covers switch to stop background and playback-screen cover lookups while Internet access is on (Settings → Advanced). The track information screen is not affected.
  • Clear the cover and image caches (Settings → Advanced).
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data stored on your device.
  • Request written confirmation that we hold no data about you by emailing the contact below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version will be published on this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Data Safety declaration on Google Play.
