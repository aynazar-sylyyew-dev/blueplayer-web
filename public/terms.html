2. What Blue Player Does

Blue Player is an offline-first music player for Android. It is designed to find and play audio files available to the App through Android's media library. Depending on the installed version and device, its features include playback, library browsing and search, playlists, favorites, bookmarks, playback queue and session restoration, playback statistics, audio settings, and audio-tag and artwork editing.

The App does not provide a music-streaming catalogue or a service for downloading songs. You select and manage the audio files available on your device.

4. Local Media, Editing, and Deletion

The App reads audio-library information provided by Android and plays accessible local files. It does not host or supply your music files.

Important: Blue Player includes functions that can change audio-file metadata and embedded artwork. It also includes a function to delete a selected track from device storage. These actions can change or remove your original files, not merely the App's display of them.

Check the selected file and the requested action before confirming an edit or deletion. Keep your own backups of important audio files. A deletion may be irreversible, and recovery may not be possible.

5. Local App Data and Settings

Blue Player stores app preferences and playback-related state locally on the device. The project uses Android SharedPreferences for settings and queue/session state, and a local SQLite database for items such as playlists, favorites, and bookmarks. Artwork and artist images downloaded for display may also be kept in the App's private local cache.

Where the settings export/import feature is available, it creates or reads a JSON file selected through Android's file picker. This is a settings backup feature; it is not cloud synchronization and it does not back up your music files.

Clearing the App's data or uninstalling it may remove locally stored settings, lists, playback state, and cached artwork. Keep separate copies of anything you need to preserve.

6. Optional Online Artwork and Third-Party Services

Network access for artwork lookup is disabled by default in the App's settings. When you enable it, Blue Player may send track titles and artist names to third-party services to search for artwork and may download returned images to the App's private local cache.

The current implementation uses the following services:

  • Apple iTunes Search API — searches for track and album artwork.
  • Deezer API — searches for track artwork and artist images.
  • MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive — search release metadata and associated cover artwork.
  • Last.fm — retrieves track and album information, including available artwork.
  • Wikipedia — retrieves artist-page thumbnail images through its API in English and Russian.

If the Wi-Fi-only option is enabled, the App limits these network requests to a Wi-Fi connection. Availability, response content, and service terms are controlled by the respective third-party providers and may change without notice.
